Animated video

What we produce

  • 60–120 second explainers that state the problem, the product and the proof, in that order.
  • Product and process animations for equipment, SaaS and manufacturing that cannot be filmed.
  • Launch films and event openers, including character-led mascot animation.
  • Cutdowns for LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube — 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 from the same master.

Animated video

How production actually runs

  • Script first. If the words do not work, no amount of motion fixes it.
  • Storyboard frames approved before animation, so there are no surprises at delivery.
  • Voice, music and sound design licensed and mixed to broadcast loudness.
  • Delivery in H.264 with captions and a project file you can re-edit.

Process

Four stages, no black boxes.

Script

Message, structure and a 90-second read-through.

Storyboard

Frame-by-frame visuals for approval before production.

Animate

Motion, character work, voice and sound design.

Deliver

Master plus platform cutdowns, captions and source files.

Investment

What it costs.

Explainer films start at ₹45,000 for 60 seconds with licensed voice and music. Character animation and 3D are quoted after storyboard.

Every engagement starts with a written scope and a fixed price, so there are no mid-project surprises.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

How long does an animated video take?

3–5 weeks for a 60–90 second explainer once the script is approved. Character animation adds 1–2 weeks.

Do you write the script?

Yes — that is where most of the value sits. We write, you review, we revise twice.

Who owns the video?

You do, including the source files. Music and stock are licensed in your name where the licence allows.

Can you match our brand style?

Yes — we work from your brand system, or build a visual direction if none exists.

Do you do live-action?

We produce animated and motion-graphic work in-house and partner for live-action shoots when a film needs both.

Talk to the team that would do the work.

Tell us the goal. We will tell you what it takes — scope, timeline and a fixed price.
