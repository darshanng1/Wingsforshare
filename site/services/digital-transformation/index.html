Why it matters

The problem we are actually solving.

Every growing business accumulates workarounds. A tool bought for one team, a spreadsheet that became critical, a legacy system only one person understands. Individually they are cheap; together they slow every process down.

We map the estate, price the friction and sequence the fixes. Some problems are solved with integration, some with automation, and a few genuinely need replacement. Saying which is which — before you spend — is most of the value.

Not sure this is the right service? Send the problem, not the brief. If a different discipline solves it faster, we will say so before quoting.

What is included

Everything in scope, listed.

  • Systems and process audit. A map of every tool, who owns it, what it costs and where data is re-entered by hand.
  • Friction and cost model. Hours lost per process, quantified — so the roadmap is ranked by payback, not by opinion.
  • Cloud migration. AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or Oracle Cloud moves with rollback plans and minimal downtime.
  • Workflow automation. Approvals, document generation, notifications and scheduled jobs replacing manual steps.
  • Integration layer. APIs and sync jobs so systems share one source of truth instead of exporting CSVs.
  • Legacy modernisation. Phased replacement of unsupported systems with a parallel-run period and data migration.
  • Change enablement. Training, SOPs and champions inside each team so adoption is not an afterthought.
  • Measurement. Before-and-after metrics on cycle time, error rate and cost per transaction.

Before you enquire

Questions we get asked

Where do we start with digital transformation?

With an audit. Two weeks mapping systems, costs and manual work usually reveals three fixes that pay for the whole engagement.

Is this only for large enterprises?

No. Businesses between 20 and 200 staff often see the fastest returns, because a single automation can remove a full-time data entry role.

Do you handle cloud migration?

Yes — AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud, with a rollback plan and a parallel-run period for anything business-critical.

How do you prove it worked?

We baseline cycle time, error rate and cost per transaction before the work starts, then report the same numbers afterwards.
