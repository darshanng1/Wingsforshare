Why it matters

The problem we are actually solving.

Most business websites fail for unglamorous reasons: pages that take six seconds to load, a theme that fights every content edit, and no clear path from a search result to an enquiry. Design is the visible part, but structure, speed and information architecture are what decide whether the site produces leads or just exists.

We build the whole stack deliberately — semantic markup, clean URL structure, structured data, image budgets and a content model your team can actually operate. That is why our builds ship SEO-ready by default instead of needing a rebuild six months later.

Not sure this is the right service? Send the problem, not the brief. If a different discipline solves it faster, we will say so before quoting.

What is included

Everything in scope, listed.

  • Custom design system. Type scale, colour tokens, components and page templates built around your brand — not a purchased theme.
  • React / Next.js front end. Component-driven front ends that stay maintainable as the site grows past twenty pages.
  • Headless CMS. Sanity, Strapi, Contentful or a WordPress headless setup so your team edits content without a developer.
  • Ecommerce capability. Shopify, WooCommerce or headless storefronts with catalog, cart and checkout flows.
  • Core Web Vitals engineering. LCP, CLS and INP brought into the green — measured on real devices, not just a lab score.
  • API and integration work. CRM, payment gateway, ERP, WhatsApp and analytics integrations wired into the build.
  • Technical SEO foundation. Sitemaps, canonicals, redirects, schema, robots rules and crawlable internal linking.
  • Handover you can use. Repository access, deployment docs, a walkthrough recording and 30 days of post-launch support.

How we deliver

Four stages, no black boxes.

Each stage ends with something you can review: a written scope, a staging link, a test build or a report. Nothing moves forward on assumption.

01

Brief and discovery

We start with the commercial goal — enquiries, orders, signups — then map the pages and flows that produce it.

02

03

Component build with accessibility, responsive and performance checks on every template before content lands.

04

Launch and handover

Redirect map, analytics verification, Search Console submission, source-code handover and a training session.

Before you enquire

Questions we get asked

How much does a business website cost?

In our published 2026 pricing bands, a professional 5–15 page business site runs ₹25,000–₹1,00,000, ecommerce and web applications ₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000, and custom platforms above that. You get a fixed written scope and price before work starts.

How long does a website take to build?

A marketing site typically ships in 2–4 weeks. Larger web apps and ecommerce stores run 4–10 weeks depending on integrations. The timeline is agreed in writing during the brief.

Do you build SEO-friendly websites?

Yes — technical SEO is part of the build, not an add-on. Semantic HTML, clean URLs, metadata, sitemap, schema, redirects and Core Web Vitals are all in scope.

Will I own the code?

Yes. You own the repository, the domain, the hosting account and the content. We hand over full source code and deployment documentation on delivery.

Can you take over an existing website?

Regularly. We audit the current build, keep what works, fix performance and SEO debt, and migrate content without losing rankings.
