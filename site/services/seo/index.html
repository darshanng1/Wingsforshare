Why it matters

The problem we are actually solving.

Search is still the highest-intent channel a business can own, and it is the only one that keeps paying after you stop spending. But most SEO retainers sell activity — a blog post a week, a report you never read — instead of movement you can trace to enquiries.

We work the other way around. Every engagement starts with a technical audit and a search-demand map, so you can see exactly which queries are worth owning, what the page needs to win them, and how long it should take. Then we execute in priority order and report against rankings, click-through and assisted enquiries.

Not sure this is the right service? Send the problem, not the brief. If a different discipline solves it faster, we will say so before quoting.

What is included

Everything in scope, listed.

  • Technical SEO audit. Crawl analysis, indexation, canonical and redirect logic, JS rendering, sitemap and robots review.
  • Keyword and intent map. Search demand clustered by intent and mapped to the pages that should own each cluster.
  • On-page optimisation. Titles, headings, internal links, entity coverage and structured data on the pages that convert.
  • Content strategy and briefs. Editorial briefs built from what actually ranks, written for buyers rather than word counts.
  • Core Web Vitals. Speed and stability work — LCP, INP and CLS treated as ranking and conversion inputs.
  • Structured data. Organization, Service, FAQ, Article, Product and Breadcrumb schema validated against rich results.
  • Local SEO. Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, location pages and review workflows.
  • Authority building. Digital PR, relevant link acquisition and partnership placements — no link farms.

Before you enquire

Questions we get asked

How long until I see SEO results?

Technical fixes and on-page work can move impressions within weeks. Competitive keyword rankings usually build over 3–6 months, and compound after that. You see progress reporting from month one.

Do you guarantee number one rankings?

No ethical agency can guarantee exact positions. We commit to a written strategy, transparent reporting and measurable organic growth — and we will tell you when a keyword is not worth chasing.

Do you do international SEO?

Yes. We handle hreflang, multi-region keyword targeting, geo-aware content and currency or language variants for businesses selling outside India.

What do you need from my team?

Access to Search Console, Analytics, the CMS and the ability to approve content. A subject-matter contact for interviews is the single biggest accelerator.

Can you work with our in-house developer?

Yes. We deliver developer-ready fix tickets with reproduction steps, and we review the implementation rather than assuming it shipped correctly.
