Brand identity

What the engagement covers

  • Positioning and naming: what you are, who it is for, and the words that make it obvious.
  • Logo system: primary mark, responsive variants, favicon, and the light/dark pairs most brands forget.
  • Type, colour and layout rules, tested on real applications — website, packaging, signage, social.
  • A voice guide with examples of how the brand writes, and how it does not.

Brand identity

Why branding fails in practice

  • A logo delivered as one PNG, then stretched, recoloured and blurred across every channel.
  • No dark-mode or small-size variant, so the mark disappears in a navbar.
  • Guidelines that are 60 pages of theory nobody opens — instead of five rules everyone follows.
  • We deliver a working kit: assets, tokens, and a one-page usage sheet the team actually uses.

Process

Four stages, no black boxes.

Discover

Positioning workshop, competitor audit, audience and language review.

Design

Two distinct directions, applied to real touchpoints before you choose.

Systemise

Final mark, type, colour, spacing and asset exports for web, print and social.

Document

A short, practical guideline plus a shared asset folder.

Investment

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Do you do naming?

Yes — shortlists with availability checks, meaning, and how each name reads aloud, alongside the visual direction.

Can you refresh an existing brand instead of starting over?

Often that is the better call. We audit what has equity and keep it, then fix the parts that break in use.

What files do we get?

SVG, PNG and WebP in light/dark variants, favicon set, type and colour tokens, and a usage sheet — plus the editable source.

How long does it take?

Typically 3–5 weeks for positioning, identity and guidelines. Naming adds 1–2 weeks.

Do you handle print and signage?

We prepare print-ready artwork and coordinate with your printer or fabricator on specs.

Talk to the team that would do the work.

Tell us the goal. We will tell you what it takes — scope, timeline and a fixed price.
