Why it matters

The problem we are actually solving.

Most ecommerce projects die on the boring details: a bloated theme, product pages with no unique copy, faceted navigation that creates thousands of duplicate URLs, and a checkout that asks for too much. Traffic arrives, and nothing converts.

We treat the catalog as an information architecture problem and checkout as a UX problem. That means deliberate category templates, per-product structured data, crawl budget spent on pages that can rank, and a payment flow tested on the devices your customers actually use.

Not sure this is the right service? Send the problem, not the brief. If a different discipline solves it faster, we will say so before quoting.

What is included

Everything in scope, listed.

  • Storefront build. Shopify, WooCommerce or a headless storefront with a custom theme — not a paid template with swapped colours.
  • Catalog architecture. Category, collection and product templates plus filtering rules that avoid duplicate-URL explosions.
  • Product and offer schema. Price, availability, reviews and variant markup so listings can earn rich results.
  • Checkout optimisation. Fewer steps, guest checkout, trust cues, address autocomplete and payment fallbacks.
  • Integrations. Razorpay, Stripe, shipping partners, GST invoicing, ERP, WhatsApp order updates.
  • Merchant Center readiness. Feed setup, taxonomy mapping and disapproved-product triage.
  • Performance for real devices. Image budgets, lazy loading and caching tuned so category pages open fast on 4G.
  • Analytics and funnels. GA4 ecommerce events, checkout-step drop-off and product-level revenue reporting.

Before you enquire

Questions we get asked

Shopify or WooCommerce — which should I pick?

Shopify if you want managed infrastructure and fast setup; WooCommerce if you need deep WordPress integration or full control of hosting. We recommend based on catalog size, integrations and who runs the store day to day.

Can you migrate an existing store without losing rankings?

Yes. We build a full redirect map from old product and category URLs, preserve metadata, and monitor Search Console for weeks after launch.

Will you set up payments and shipping?

Yes — gateways, shipping partners, GST invoicing and notification flows are part of the build, tested with real orders before launch.

Do you optimise for conversion after launch?

We run a post-launch CRO pass and can continue with a monthly merchandising and funnel retainer.
