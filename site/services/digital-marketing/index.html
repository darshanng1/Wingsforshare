FAQ

Questions we get asked

How much ad budget do I need?

For Bangalore B2B services, ₹40,000–₹1,00,000 per month in media spend is a realistic starting point to gather statistically useful data. We will tell you if your budget is too low to compete.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. We quote a fixed monthly fee, so there is no incentive to inflate your spend.

How quickly do leads come in?

Paid campaigns can produce enquiries within the first two weeks. Organic and content work compound over 3–6 months.

Do you write the creative?

Yes — ad copy, landing pages, and coordination with design for static and video assets.

What do you need from us?

Access to ad accounts, a sales contact who can respond to leads within a day, and one decision-maker for approvals.
