What working together actually looks like.

Four stages, each ending with something you can review. No ambiguous discovery phase, no invoice before scope, no “trust us” in the middle.

3–7 days
01

Audit

We look at the business, not just the brief: current site, search visibility, competitors, tracking and the manual work your team does around it.

3–5 days
02

Roadmap

A written plan with scope, sequence, timeline and fixed price. You see exactly what ships in phase one and what waits.

2–20 weeks
03

Build

Design and build in two-week increments with staging links you can open, click and critique before anything goes live.

Ongoing
04

Measure

Analytics verified, Search Console submitted, and a monthly review of what moved and what we do next.

Working rhythm

Weekly, not monthly.

Long silences are where projects go wrong. You get a short written update every week: what shipped, what is blocked, what is next.

Staging links, not screenshots

Every build is reviewable in the browser from the first template. Feedback lands against the real thing.

One accountable contact

You talk to the person who understands the build, not an account manager relaying messages.

Change control in writing

New requests get a price and a timeline impact before anyone builds them. Nothing appears on an invoice unexplained.

Launch is a checklist

Redirects, analytics, Search Console, backups, DNS and rollback plan — verified in order, not remembered.

After launch

The first 30 days decide whether it works.

Launch is when real traffic, real devices and real edge cases arrive. We stay on for the first month to fix defects, watch analytics and tune what the data shows.

Scheduling

Timelines and availability

How soon can we start?

Usually within one to two weeks of a signed scope, depending on the current queue. Audits can often start sooner.

How much of our time will this take?

Roughly two to three hours in week one for the brief, then one 30-minute review per sprint. Content and approvals are the main ongoing requirement.

What if we need to pause?

Tell us in writing. We will park the work at the next clean milestone and document exactly where things stand.

Do you work with our existing developer or agency?

Yes. We can lead, work alongside, or hand over with documentation. We do not insist on owning the relationship.
