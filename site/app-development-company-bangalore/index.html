FAQ

Questions we get asked

React Native or native — which do you recommend?

For most business apps, React Native gives one codebase and faster delivery. We recommend native only when the app depends on heavy device features or platform-specific performance.

How long does an MVP take?

6–12 weeks for a focused MVP with login, core workflow, payments and an admin view.

Do you handle Play Store and App Store submission?

Yes — builds, store listings, privacy declarations and review responses are included.

Who owns the code?

You do. Repository, credentials and documentation are handed over at the end of the engagement.

Can you take over an unfinished app?

Yes. We audit the existing codebase first and give you an honest assessment before quoting.
