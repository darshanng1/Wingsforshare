Privacy notice

What this website collects, what we do with an enquiry, and how to have your data removed. Last updated 15 November 2026.

What this website collects

This site uses Ahrefs Web Analytics — a cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics script. It records aggregate page views and referrers. It does not set advertising cookies, build a profile of you, or track you across other websites.

The project brief form does not submit to a server: it composes an email in your own mail client, so nothing is transmitted until you press send.

What happens when you contact us

When you email, call or message us, we receive the details you choose to share — typically your name, contact details, company and project information. We use that information only to respond to your enquiry and to deliver work you engage us for.

  • We do not sell or rent contact data.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list without an explicit request.
  • Project files and credentials are shared through access-controlled systems, not email attachments where avoidable.

Client project data

For client projects we work under the terms of the signed agreement and any data processing addendum. Access to production systems is limited to the people delivering the work, credentials are stored in a secrets manager, and access is revoked at handover or on request.

Retention

Enquiry correspondence is retained for up to 24 months so we can pick up a conversation where it left off. Invoices and statutory records are retained as required by Indian law. You can ask us to delete enquiry data at any time.

Analytics

Page-view analytics are provided by Ahrefs Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not collect personal identifiers. Because no cookie is set, there is no consent banner to dismiss. If you block the script with an extension or browser setting, the site continues to work normally.

Your rights

Write to info@wingsforshare.com to request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. We respond within 30 days.
