Engagement models

Three ways to work with us.

Most clients start fixed-scope and move to a retainer once the foundation is in place.

Most common

Fixed-scope project

A written scope, timeline and price agreed before work starts. Best for websites, stores, MVPs and dashboards with a clear finish line.

Growth

Monthly retainer

A block of senior time each month across SEO, content, development and reporting. Best when the goal is continuous growth rather than one deliverable.

What moves the number

Four things, not page count.

If a quote is much lower than these bands for the same scope, something is being left out — usually testing, SEO foundations, or the person who fixes it later.

1. Custom design vs template

Custom means the layout serves your sales process. Templates mean someone else’s decisions with your logo on top.

2. Content readiness

Copywriting, product data and photography are the most underestimated line items in any build.

3. Integrations

Payments, CRM, ERP, shipping and messaging. Each one adds scope — and each should map to an outcome.

4. Who builds it

Agency cost buys design, engineering, SEO and QA as separate disciplines. A single freelancer cannot cover all four at once.

Pricing FAQ

The awkward questions, answered.

Money

On quotes and payments

Why publish price bands at all?

Because vague pricing wastes everyone’s time. These are the bands from our published 2026 guide, and they are what we quote from. A specific proposal narrows the band once scope is clear.

Is the price fixed after the proposal?

Yes. Once the scope is signed, the price is fixed for that scope. If you add requirements mid-build, we price the change separately and you approve it before work starts.

What is not included?

Third-party costs — domain, hosting, paid media budget, stock photography, paid plugins and app store fees. We list them transparently and can manage them on your behalf at cost.

Do you offer payment plans?

Projects are split into milestone payments, typically a deposit, one or two build milestones, and the balance on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

Can we start with something small?

Yes. An audit, a single landing page or one dashboard module are all legitimate first engagements — and often the fastest way to test how we work.
