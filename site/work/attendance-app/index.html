Overview

Attendance software usually assumes everyone badges into the same door. For field services, sales teams, logistics and maintenance crews, that assumption breaks immediately.

Wings Attendance is built around the opposite assumption: people work in many places, managers need proof and context, and HR needs payroll-ready records without chasing spreadsheets.

The constraint

Geo-fenced check-in that cannot be gamed, live location without draining battery, and a reporting layer HR can export for payroll — across many industries and shift patterns.

Approach

What we decided, and why.

Every build is a set of trade-offs. These were the ones that shaped this product.

01

Verified check-in

Location-fenced attendance with device context, so a check-in means the person was actually on site.

02

Team structure

Groups, shifts and reporting lines that match how the business is actually organised.

03

Live visibility

Manager view of where teams are and what has been completed today.

04

Payroll-ready output

Attendance, leave and overtime summarised in an export HR can process directly.

What shipped

Capabilities in the build.

  • Attendance and shift tracking
  • Employee profiles and HR records
  • Daily work reports
  • Team and group management
  • In-app group chat
  • Live GPS tracking with geo-fencing
  • Leave and payroll-ready summaries
  • Works across industries and shift patterns

What changed

The operational difference.

Field check-ins are verified against location instead of trusted on word.

Managers see daily progress without calling each team.

HR exports attendance and leave for payroll without rebuilding a spreadsheet each month.
