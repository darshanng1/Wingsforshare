Vastra — Garment Ecommerce Store

A clothing store where size, fit and fabric are answered on the product page — the three questions that decide whether a first-time buyer checks out.

Overview

Fashion ecommerce lives or dies on the product page. If a shopper cannot answer "will this fit me" and "what is it made of", the cart is abandoned regardless of how the homepage looks.

We built the store around that decision: a mobile-first product template, size guidance above the fold, and a checkout flow short enough to finish on a phone with one hand.

The constraint

A catalog with many variants and thin product copy, plus a customer base that browses almost entirely on mobile over average connections.

Approach

What we decided, and why.

Every build is a set of trade-offs. These were the ones that shaped this product.

01

Mobile-first templates

Product, collection and cart templates designed at 390px first, then scaled up.

02

Variant clarity

Size, colour and stock state surfaced before the add-to-cart decision, not after.

03

Checkout friction audit

Removed unnecessary fields, added guest checkout and clear delivery expectations.

04

Performance budget

Compressed imagery and lazy loading to keep collection pages fast on mobile data.

What shipped

Capabilities in the build.

  • Product catalog with variants
  • Cart and checkout flow
  • Collection pages with filters
  • Size and fit guidance blocks
  • Order confirmation flow
  • Mobile-first responsive layout
  • Product structured data
  • Conversion-focused product template

What changed

The operational difference.

Product pages answer fit, fabric and delivery questions without leaving the page.

A single mobile checkout path with fewer fields and clearer expectations.

Collection browsing stays usable with a large variant-heavy catalog.
