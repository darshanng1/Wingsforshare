GardenWalla Nursery Store

A plant nursery moved its catalog online with category browsing, seasonal collections and an ordering flow simple enough for first-time buyers.

Overview

Nursery customers buy by browsing, not by searching. They need to see what is in season, what suits their light and space, and what will survive delivery.

We built a browsing-led store with clear categories, care details on the product page and an ordering flow that sets expectations about plant size and delivery.

The constraint

Large, visually inconsistent inventory, live plants with care requirements, and customers unfamiliar with buying plants online.

Approach

What we decided, and why.

Every build is a set of trade-offs. These were the ones that shaped this product.

01

Browsing over search

Category-first navigation by plant type, season and care level.

02

Care information up front

Light, water and size guidance on the product page to reduce returns and support questions.

03

Simple ordering

A short add-to-cart path with clear delivery expectations for living products.

What shipped

Capabilities in the build.

  • Category-based browsing
  • Seasonal collections
  • Care and size details
  • Add to cart and ordering flow
  • Mobile-optimised layout
  • Fast-loading catalog pages

What changed

The operational difference.

The catalog is browsable by plant type and season rather than a flat product list.

Care guidance on the product page reduces pre-sale questions.

Ordering works on a phone for customers new to buying plants online.
