Terms of service

The working terms for engagements with WingsForShare Digital Solutions. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Proposals and scope

Work begins after a written proposal is accepted. The proposal defines the deliverables, timeline and price for that scope. Requirements added after acceptance are quoted separately and approved before they are built.

Payment

Projects are invoiced in milestones — typically a deposit to begin, one or two milestone payments during the build, and the balance on delivery. Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance. Invoices are payable within the period stated on the invoice.

Intellectual property

On full payment, ownership of the custom code, designs and content produced for your project transfers to you. We retain the right to reference the work publicly unless you ask us not to. Third-party components remain under their own licences, which we list at handover.
