A small senior team, deliberately.

WingsForShare Digital Solutions is a private digital agency based in Bangalore. We build websites, stores, applications and data systems, and we run the search and analytics work that makes them pay for themselves.

Who we are

Builders who got tired of watching good sites fail.

Most of our work starts the same way: a business has a website, sometimes a good-looking one, and it is not producing enquiries. The design is fine. The problem is underneath — slow pages, no clear structure for search, a CMS the team cannot use, tracking nobody configured.

So we became the team that handles the whole stack. Design, engineering, search, analytics and the operations software behind the business. Nine disciplines, one accountable group of people, and a written scope before anything starts.

We are based in Bangalore and work with clients in India, the US, the UK, Australia and the Middle East. Delivery is remote-first, communication is in English, and the code we write belongs to you.

Principles

Four rules we do not negotiate.

These decide how we scope, build and hand over. If a client wants the opposite, we are the wrong agency — and we will say so early.

01

Scope before pixels

We agree what the work has to achieve, and what it costs, before anyone opens a design tool. Ambiguity is expensive later.

02

Build for the operator

A site nobody can edit, a dashboard nobody trusts and an app nobody opens are all failed builds — however good the screenshots look.

03

Measure or it did not happen

Analytics and tracking are configured before launch, not bolted on when someone asks for a report.

04

Own your foundations

You keep the code, the domain and the accounts. We would rather be retained for the next phase than hold your assets hostage.

How we work

You will always know the next step.

Staging links you can open, a written scope before the invoice, and one person accountable for the answer. If something slips, you hear it from us first.

See the delivery process ↗

01 · Audit — 3–7 days

We look at the business, not just the brief: current site, search visibility, competitors, tracking and the manual work your team does around it.

02 · Roadmap — 3–5 days

A written plan with scope, sequence, timeline and fixed price. You see exactly what ships in phase one and what waits.

03 · Build — 2–20 weeks

Design and build in two-week increments with staging links you can open, click and critique before anything goes live.

04 · Measure — Ongoing

Analytics verified, Search Console submitted, and a monthly review of what moved and what we do next.
