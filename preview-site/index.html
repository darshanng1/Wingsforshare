Questions, answered.

The things clients ask before they start. If yours isn't here, email us.

What does WingsForShare do?

We are a global digital agency. We deliver web development, SEO, social media marketing, mobile app development and business analytics — everything you need to grow online, under one roof.

Do you work with clients worldwide?

Yes. We work with businesses across the US, UK, Europe, Australia, the Middle East and India, and we optimise for global as well as local search rankings.

How long does a project take?

A marketing website typically ships in 2–4 weeks; web apps and ecommerce in 4–10 weeks; mobile apps and SaaS MVPs in 6–12 weeks. You get a fixed timeline before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped in writing. A brochure site and a custom platform are different investments. After a short discovery call, we give you a fixed quote with the scope attached.

Do you work with existing teams?

Yes. We can lead the build, embed with your in-house team, or take a defined slice of the work. The scope and handover are agreed up front.

How do we get started?

Send a brief through the project form or email info@wingsforshare.com. We reply within one working day with a plan, timeline and fixed quote — no obligation.
