## Rules applied
- **One primary keyword per page**; never make two pages compete for the same term (keyword cannibalisation).
- **Global phrasing** — "agency/services/company" without a city; add geo only for dedicated location pages later.
- **Buyer intent first** — service pages target commercial terms; the blog targets informational terms and funnels to the matching service page.
- **Long-tail = the blog** — each cluster above gets articles (see `/blog`).
